About SecSell

SecSell is built for players who want a simple, clear and fast order process for Driving Empire cash packages. The page shows the package amount, the current price, the available stock and the estimated delivery time before the customer reports a payment.

The idea is simple: choose a package, select the delivery method, report the payment, and then follow the progress inside My Orders. Admins can update every order with a status such as payment pending, paid, in progress, delivered or cancelled. That makes the process easier to understand for both the customer and the admin team.

Support is handled through the live chat and the support page. Customers can ask questions before or after ordering, and admins can answer from the Admin panel. The system is connected to the backend, so important shop data like sales, announcements, reviews, orders and chat messages is shared between devices instead of only living in one browser.

Clear package cards

Every card shows price, stock, wait time, sale labels and sale countdowns when an offer is active.

Backend powered

Sales, announcements, orders, reviews and chat messages are saved on the server so visitors see the same shop data.

Admin workflow

The admin panel is made for quick order handling: add notes, mark as paid, mark as in progress, mark as delivered or delete wrong orders.

FAQ

How long does delivery take?

Small packages usually take around 1 day. Large packages can take up to 5 business days.

How do I confirm payment?

Select a package, choose payment, then click “Report Payment”. Your order appears in My Orders.

Where do I see updates?

Open My Orders. Admin can change your status from pending to delivered.

Can I use live chat?

Yes, choose live chat delivery or open Support. The admin can reply from the Admin chat panel.
